Cookies and storage

This website does not use cookies, neither its own nor advertising ones.

Measurement: I use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to know how many visits the site gets and how fast it loads. They work without cookies, don’t store your IP or create a persistent identifier: they only count aggregated, anonymous data (page visited, country, browser and load times), and they’re served from this same website.

To make the experience work, your browser keeps a few preferences you create in its local storage: your chosen language, unlocked achievements and your arcade high score. They never leave your device and you can clear them from your browser settings.

Fonts and icons are served from this website, with no calls to external services.

Third-party content: the Spotify player only loads if you press the button to listen. From then on, Spotify may use its own cookies under its cookie policy.
